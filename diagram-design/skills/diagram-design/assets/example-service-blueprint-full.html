THE HEADLINE

One check below the line decides the whole journey

Everything above the line of visibility is polish. The KYC check is the only step that can hold an application for days, and the customer never sees it — which is exactly why it earns the coral.

Bands are fixed, not actors

  • Four layers, always in this order
  • Boundaries are named, not just drawn
  • Line of visibility reads strongest

Support means systems

The bottom band holds dependencies the service leans on — a vendor API and the core ledger. Nobody works there; they are drawn as systems, never as people.
