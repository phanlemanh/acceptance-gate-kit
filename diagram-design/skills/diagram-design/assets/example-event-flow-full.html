Order pipeline events

Checkout and payment announce facts to the broker; fulfillment, notifications and analytics each react on their own schedule. Whatever fulfillment cannot process drops out of the topology into a dead-letter queue.

THE RISK

Failure needs an address

The dashed path is the only place a message can go when fulfillment rejects it. Nothing subscribes to orders.dlq — that is the point, and the operational debt.

Topics absorb the coupling

  • Checkout never names its readers
  • Analytics batches without slowing anyone
  • Fulfillment subscribes to two topics, once

Past tense, on purpose

Every channel names a fact that already happened. An imperative name like createOrder would be a command — and belong on a sequence diagram instead.
