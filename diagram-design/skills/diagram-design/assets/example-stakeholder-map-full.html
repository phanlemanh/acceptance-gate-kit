Who surrounds the data platform

An orbit map for an internal data-platform launch — nine parties placed by proximity, not priority. Ring says how close; sector says which side of the wall; coral says whose support decides the outcome.

READING THE MAP

Proximity, not priority

Rings answer how close a party sits to the work. If the question were power against interest, this would be a quadrant instead.

The wall is the second axis

  • Internal parties share the roadmap
  • External parties share a contract
  • Escalation paths differ on each side

Two names to win

The CDO sponsor funds the roadmap and Security signs off each release. Everyone else is briefed; these two decide.
