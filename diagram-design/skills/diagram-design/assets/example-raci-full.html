Feature launch RACI

Who does what when a feature ships: six activities from spec to post-launch review, five roles, and exactly one accountable owner per row. Empty cells are deliberate — a role with no stake stays silent.

THE HEADLINE

One owner per row — except in practice

Every activity resolves to a single accountable role on paper, but launch comms is contested: Marketing holds the A while the PM still edits every note. The matrix makes the dispute visible instead of hiding it in meetings.

Accountability rotates

  • PM owns spec and the post-launch review.
  • Design, Eng, and QA each own their craft stage.
  • R appears only where the doer differs from the owner.

Silence is honest

Five cells stay empty. A role with no stake in an activity gets no letter — filling every cell with C or I is how RACI charts stop meaning anything.
